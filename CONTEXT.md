# ASCII wallpaper

A wallpaper combines an ASCII logo and text on a background for use as a desktop image.

## Language

**Wallpaper**:
A desktop background image containing an ASCII logo and accompanying text.

**ASCII logo**:
A recognizable symbol drawn with rows of ASCII characters, preserving their spacing and alignment.
_Avoid_: Logo image, raster logo

**Text panel**:
An ordered collection of label/value rows accompanying the ASCII logo.
_Avoid_: System report, detected specifications

**Label/value row**:
A named piece of information and its manually supplied value in the text panel.

**Color band**:
A horizontal region of the ASCII logo whose characters share one color.

**Palette**:
An ordered collection of colors used for the ASCII logo's color bands.

**Composition**:
The ASCII logo and text panel arranged together as one visual block within the wallpaper.

**Preset**:
A ready-to-use selection of ASCII logo, text panel, and appearance settings.
